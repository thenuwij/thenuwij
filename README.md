## Hi, I'm Thenuja 👋

I'm a Computer Engineering (Honours) graduate from UNSW Sydney, now working there as a Research Assistant. I build backend systems and applied AI, and I care most about reliability. I like designing services that keep working when parts of them fail, and I enjoy the work of running them in production, from deployment and monitoring to testing the failure cases on purpose.

Most of my work sits across the full stack. I write Python and FastAPI backends on PostgreSQL, build React frontends, and deploy to AWS. I also have a background in machine learning, including computer vision models in PyTorch, and in embedded hardware, including FPGA design in VHDL. That mix keeps me comfortable moving between high-level application logic and the layers underneath it.

My main project is **[UniVise](https://github.com/thenuwij/UniVise)**, the platform I built for my Honours thesis. It helps university students plan their courses, majors and career pathways, and I focused on the backend logic and the AI integration. It received a High Distinction and a place in the UNSW CSE Thesis Showcase 2025. It is now university-funded, so I maintain it for its student test users, with a usability study coming up.

I'm always happy to talk about backend engineering, applied AI and building things that hold up in production. Let's connect!
